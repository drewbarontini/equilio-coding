## Limits

These are individual observed conversations, not a reliability estimate. Deliberate invocation does not establish automatic selection quality. Local installation verifies packaging for both clients; it does not establish Claude Code's runtime review behavior.

Offline exports do not test authenticated GitHub access, pagination, live CI, thread resolution APIs, revision races, posting authorization, or submitted reviews. The cases do not cover every recommendation outcome, a multi-checkpoint conversation, or real deployed provider/cache behavior. The subsequent-review case checks current concerns despite a missing intermediate revision; it cannot prove an actual r2→r3 inspection.
